## Facts (per shrine's own official account, per owner)

- 坐摩神社 origin tradition: 神功皇后 returned from Silla and dedicated 坐摩大神
  at 大江・田蓑島、のちの渡辺（現在の天満橋西方・石町付近）, south bank of the Yodo.
- Five deities (総称 for 坐摩大神): 生井神・福井神・綱長井神・**波比岐神**・阿須波神
  (page had 波比祇神 ×3 — wrong).
- Present shrine: 大阪市中央区久太郎町. Former site: 石町, where **坐摩神社元宮・行宮**
  (大阪市中央区石町2-2-15) still stands — the physical landmark for the Jingū tradition.
  Present shrine and 行宮 = two related but distinct locations.
- Ordered to vacate (替地) for Hideyoshi's Osaka Castle construction; moved to present
  site in the **寛永年間** (not "moved 1608 with the castle build").
- Do NOT describe the shrine as simply "Jingū's shrine" — Jingū belongs to the
  shrine's origin tradition; five 坐摩大神 are the principal deities.
